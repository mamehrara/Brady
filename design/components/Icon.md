# Icon

Brady's small outlined icon set, inheriting `color` from its parent.

**You provide** `name` (posture, move, eyes, water, stretch, snooze, check, close), `size` (14 in chips, 16 in buttons, 24 elsewhere) and `label` when the icon stands alone.

**Do**
- Pair it with a word, or give it a `label`.

**Don't**
- Mix in icons from other sets; ask for a new one drawn to the same 24px grid and 1.75px stroke.
