# Chip

A small pill naming a reminder kind with its icon and tone, so a glance tells Posture from Move without reading.

**You provide** `kind`; `children` overrides the default word (Posture, Move, Eyes, Water, Stretch).

**Do**
- Use it in the toast head and in history lists.
- Keep the icon; Eyes and Water share the `sky` tone and differ only by icon and word.

**Don't**
- Make chips clickable or use them for anything but a reminder kind.
