# Mascot

Brady himself, in the `hang` pose (arms up, claws over an edge) or the `head` pose (avatar and tray), in one of three moods.

**You provide** `pose` (`hang` or `head`), `mood` (`awake` for a new reminder, `cheer` after Done, `sleepy` after Snooze or while paused), `size` in px, and an optional `label` for screen readers.

**Do**
- Hang him from a top edge, with the claws over it.
- Change his mood to answer what the user just did.

**Don't**
- Recolor, flip, crop or stretch him, or put text over him.
- Show more than one Brady on screen at a time.
