# Logos

- `brady-mark.svg` — Brady's head with his leaf, full color. The tray icon, the app avatar and the Settings header. Give it clear space of a quarter of its width; never on a busy photo.
- `brady-app-icon.svg` — The mark on a `moss` rounded square, with a lighter leaf. The Windows app and Start menu icon; export to .ico at 16, 24, 32, 48 and 256.

There is no wordmark: set "Brady" in Fredoka 600 (`display` family) beside the mark.
